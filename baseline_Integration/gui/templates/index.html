Multi-Attribute Fusion Layout & CKKS 4096-Slot Packing

Schema & vector layout · cross-script multi-attribute design

Classic fuzzy matching handles a single Latin name field and breaks on homophone typos or cross-script romanization. This demo fuses several attributes into one vector: each attribute is encoded independently, then the parts are concatenated, so a single encrypted inner product scores all attributes at once.

Step 1 — Offline Encoding & Clustering

Party B · offline preprocessing · done once

Party B turns every database record into a fused multi-attribute vector and groups the vectors into K clusters with cosine K-Means, producing the centroid matrix and the column-wise store used later.

Privacy: this step runs entirely on Party B's side. No network exchange happens and no plaintext ever leaves B.

Step 4 — Centroid Scores & Probe Selection

Party A · online · pick the top-K probe clusters

Party B homomorphically scores the encrypted query against every centroid; Party A decrypts the K similarity values. To avoid missing borderline records, the system probes the few closest clusters (multi-cluster probing) instead of only the best one, and scans them in order.

Step 9 — Column Scan & Early Stop

Party A · online · encrypted verdict with sublinear early stop

Inside each probed cluster, Party B blinds every candidate column with a positive random mask and a threshold shift: mask · (score − τ). Party A decrypts and declares a hit (CATCH) on the first positive value, then stops immediately — the remaining candidates are skipped, so a typical query scans only a small fraction of the database.
